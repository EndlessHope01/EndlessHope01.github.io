About Us

Game Hub is a student-run gaming news website created by two technology students at the Canadian Institute of Technology. What started as a university project quickly turned into something we genuinely enjoy — a place where gamers can find the latest news, honest reviews, and useful guides all in one spot. We cover everything from AAA releases and esports highlights to indie gems and hardware reviews. If it's happening in the gaming world, we're talking about it.

Why We Built This

We're both gamers first, students second. Between lectures and assignments, gaming is how we unwind — and we found ourselves constantly jumping between five different websites just to stay up to date. Game Hub is our answer to that problem. One site, everything you need.

What We Cover

  • News — Breaking stories and announcements from the gaming industry
  • Reviews — Honest, no-nonsense reviews of the latest releases
  • Guides — Tips, walkthroughs, and builds to help you play better
  • Hardware — The best gear for every budget
  • Esports — Tournament results, team news, and competitive coverage
